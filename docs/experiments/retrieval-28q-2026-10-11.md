# B0, R1, R3 on 28 Development Questions (2026-10-11)

Development set after adding Apple and Walmart: 28 questions on three companies (Berkshire Hathaway, Apple, Walmart), all confirmed by the project owner (single reviewer). Denominators: 20 answerable, 4 insufficient-evidence, 4 clarification. One answerable question is 5 percentage points. Coca-Cola, JPMorgan, and Nike are reserved for final evaluation and were not used.

## Conditions

All three runs used commit `29e704c` with a clean worktree, three repeats, the same prompt (`b0-v2-4a58d1dde6ff`), `gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17`, bge-small, top-5, and the same organization A member. Runs: `20261011T032310Z-B0`, `20261011T032512Z-R1`, `20261011T032810Z-R3` (local, Git-ignored). Cost about $0.56 (494k input, 41k output tokens). R2 (hybrid over B0 chunks) was measured only by the retrieval diagnostic, since it scored 0% on the first 12 questions.

## Errors

| Kind | Examples | Configs |
|---|---|---|
| **Arithmetic** with correct inputs | dev-17: inputs 416,161 and 391,035, answer 6.42% (exact 6.4255 → 6.43); dev-25: 713,163 and 680,985, answer 4.72% (exact 4.7252 → 4.73) | R3 (4 of 6 runs), B0, R1 |
| Wrong table, confident answer | dev-03: 7,611 instead of 49,196 in every R3 run; dev-05/06: segment totals in R1 | R1, R3 |
| Evidence not retrieved, abstained | dev-14 (Services row), dev-15/18/25/26 in R1, dev-08 | R1, R3 |
| Clarification missed | dev-28 answered fiscal 2025 only; dev-20 abstained; dev-11 used a pro forma figure | all |
| Out-of-scope answered as clarification | dev-19 (Apple fiscal 2026) asked "fiscal 2025 or 2026?" in one R3 run | R3 |

The arithmetic cases are the clearest new finding: retrieval and extraction were right, and the model's percentage was off by one in the second decimal. That is the error N2 (deterministic Decimal calculation on the same inputs) targets.

## Decision

- **Use R3 (table chunks + hybrid retrieval) for the next experiments.** On 28 questions it leads on recall, accuracy, and false deferral, and also under an equal token budget. Earlier, on 12 Berkshire-only questions, R1 led under the equal budget; the reversal is a reminder that 12 questions were not enough to decide.
- Keep B0 as the baseline. The API default stays B0 until the answer path is finalized.
- Next: G1/G2 for wrong-table answers and clarification behavior, N1/N2 for arithmetic, each compared with R3 on these 28 questions. The final evaluation questions for Coca-Cola, JPMorgan, and Nike stay unwritten until the configuration is frozen.
